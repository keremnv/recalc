Rule: a claim belongs to the narrowest product component and execution regime
whose integrated implementation directly inherited or reproduced the supporting
evidence. Mechanism evidence never attaches broader than the population and
boundary it tested.

4.5 GHz P-core; governor powersave, not fixed; no exclusive-core claim).

## Populations and boundaries (do not mix)

- Fixed-22 contact-selected: direct-contact mechanism view. Ratios here (0.468)
  describe the earned surface, not the product.
- Representative-30 (22 ref-only / 7 direct / 1 fallback): fixed product-boundary
  view across 21 tasks. Not a market-prevalence estimate.
- Changed-5: assurance-correctness view; timing (+107.7 ms) is charged cost.
- Phase-10B 22 reference-only: transparency view under two run protocols
  (unpinned +18.85 ms archived; pinned +6.39 ms). Never average across protocols.
- Session economics (N=1..5, each from cold artifact): descriptive only;
  no horizon extrapolated.
