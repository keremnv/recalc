**Historical ordinary-baseline inference.** P1 contains 183 archived submitted outputs on 98 tasks. The defensible conclusion is “no demonstrated cache-only alteration of the retained archived ordinary submission baseline under this replay.”

That is narrower than “ordinary agents were unaffected by cache loss.” Every retained P1 agent-output hash differs from its archived submitted-output hash. This proves that raw agent outputs and scored submissions are different artifacts; the hash difference alone does not prove the transformation was exclusively recalculation. The populated submitted caches and lack of further recovery are consistent with prior processing having already supplied the relevant caches. They cannot estimate the effect of processing the distinct raw outputs. Joining an unrefreshed agent output to its processed submission's historical score would conflate these stages.
