# Historical token evidence reconstructed

`historical_token_summary.json` now contains the original 15 aggregate/cohort entries plus 168 task-arm records from the representative checkpoint and the two completed token discovery studies. It retains unavailable splits as null, and the raw study ledgers remain the authority for per-call usage. Cohorts overlap, so their aggregate rows are not independent replications.

The representative checkpoint is useful for workload scale and paired log-ratio variance, but its H1 runtime/scaffold and noncompletion differences prevent attributing token changes to inspection salience. Thin-checkpoint and inspection-efficiency totals suggest that view behavior can change, yet they bundle visible notes with runtime/helper changes and sometimes include repeated discordant pairs. Batch-write and deterministic-execution experiments had lower treatment tokens with zero relevant helper/executor use; those disconfirm an execution-only interpretation. Other runtime and representative comparisons showed higher treatment totals. The first claim-specific 2×2 is the most informative causal contrast for the proposed product-facing profile, while the later fresh affordance study tests whether a helper cue or announced availability replicates; both are negative for a stable product claim.

Mechanically, the first 2×2's D/A uncensored geometric input ratio was 0.854, decomposed into a geometric call-count ratio of 0.821 and input-per-call ratio of 1.041. B/A was 0.706 = 0.754 calls × 0.937 input per call. Broad `view_xlsx` use fell, but visible observation bytes increased in D versus A (1,008,265 versus 957,611), as did Python stdout and input tokens per call. The apparent reduction is therefore primarily shorter trajectories, with unresolved causes. Helper execution occurred in one B run and no D run, and inspection displacement was not established.

Family behavior was heterogeneous: the full-surface result was favorable in Template, near even in Financial_Model, and adverse in Debugging. `Template:01_05` and `Template:04_04` dominate D/A; after omitting those two as a **sensitivity analysis**, the remaining 12 uncensored pairs have median ratio 1.034 and geometric ratio 1.096. `Financial_Model:15_03` and several Debugging runs consumed over a million input tokens while failing to submit. The later affordance study had only two or three dual-valid pairs per contrast and provider-routing differences on influential tasks. Those limitations make a representative percentage claim indefensible.

The live 2×2 could discriminate an observation-size pathway from a call-count pathway because it archived per-request usage and tool observations. It did so: the predicted size pathway failed. It could not identify helper execution causality because H bundled note and availability, adoption was sparse, and the one adopter was endogenous. The later affordance study addressed part of that bundle but had no factual helper execution and too few comparable successful pairs.
